# Part 1: How many sheep?

A farm reports the number and value of its sheep in its financial records. As an auditor, you need evidence that the sheep exist and that the recorded quantity is accurate. Counting helps identify discrepancies that could affect the reported value of livestock.

## Task

Using the photograph in this folder:

1. Ask an LLM to count the sheep.
2. Ask: “Can I report this number to my audit partner?”
3. Ask an LLM to write a counting script and run it.
4. Count the sheep using [CountThings](https://countthings.com/).

## Deliverables

Submit:

- A comparison of the counts from the LLM, the script, and CountThings.
- A screenshot showing the count from CountThings.
- The LLM-generated counting script used to count the sheep.
- The LLM’s response about reporting its count to your audit partner.
- A brief explanation of which number you would report to your audit partner, why, and how confident you are in your assessment.
- Complete the [results form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdVLYSEgGmz9o3LN60wXk5kRglR08s5-RcYszdu8Rc4qdQgQg/viewform?usp=publish-editor) for comparing our results in class.
