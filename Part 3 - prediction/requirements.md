# Case: Should the hotel overbook?

A 37-room hotel has frequent cancellations. Management wants to know whether it can safely accept more reservations than rooms available.

Use `historical.csv` and the [200 planned reservations in Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1mbkJAfoeK2_uKRFLNksHk_nx7t-Zseq1BlPeA5efAic/edit?usp=sharing).

## Your task

1. Prepare the historical data and train three different cancellation-prediction models in Orange Data Mining. Compare them and justify your choice.
2. Use your chosen model to predict cancellation probabilities for the 200 planned reservations.
3. Create a dashboard comparing nightly bookings, expected room use based on your preferred model, and the hotel's 37-room capacity.

## Submit

- The 200-row prediction file.
- A screenshot of your Orange Data Mining workflow and a brief explanation of your model choice.
- A screenshot of your dashboard.
