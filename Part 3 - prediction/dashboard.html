Why Random Forest?

ModelAUCAccuracyF1MCC

Random Forest has the highest AUC (0.871), accuracy (79.5%), F1 (0.793), and MCC (0.568) in the supplied Orange results. These classification metrics do not establish probability calibration. Scores are transcribed from the user’s actual results screenshot.

Management recommendation

Review the existing overbooking on 28 November and maintain a contingency for at least one room if every booked guest arrives. The model predicts substantial spare capacity, so additional reservations could be considered after validating the probabilities. Do not use the difference between expected occupancy and 37 as an automatic booking allowance. Define an acceptable relocation risk, check cancellation patterns and any other bookings, and account for compensation costs before setting an overbooking limit.

Forecast limits: historical records are from 2015–2016, while planned arrivals are in 2026. The future dataset’s mean predicted cancellation rate (62.6%) exceeds the historical observed rate (40.2%). Differences in season and customer mix may contribute; probability calibration and transfer to current conditions have not been verified. Cross-validation randomly splits historical records and is not a test on future 2026 outcomes.
